# Product requirement traceability

Source: **VS_PartnerHub_Enterprise_Product_Documentation (1).docx**, version 1.0, and the accompanying 22-section specification and follow-up requirements. The original confidential document is not committed to this repository. This matrix includes the document’s foundation, enterprise operations and intelligence roadmap.

“Implemented” below describes a persisted, authorized application workflow. It does not mean that an external provider, production infrastructure or native distribution has been provisioned. See the explicit integration boundaries at the end and the [verification evidence](VERIFICATION.md).

## Intelligence, architecture and user experience — document sections 15–18

The reporting layer now has dedicated Executive, Partner, Procurement, Performance, Recruitment, Finance, Compliance and Support views. It includes KPI definitions, current verification stages, evidence-based response/delivery rates, invoice aging, required-document gaps, actual CSV/PDF exports and scoped Power Query/API connections. The generated schema/payload/KPI dictionaries and external Power BI/Tableau setup are documented in [ANALYTICS.md](ANALYTICS.md). External BI tenant publication is an operator integration step.

The public workspace tour pins the heading and card together and advances through all nine organization types on desktop and portrait phones, with reverse scrolling and direct touch/keyboard tabs. Compact short-screen layouts retain the heading and readable controls; reduced-motion and insufficient landscape space use ordinary tabs. Resizing preserves selection and keeps the selected tab visible. The group uses its content height, compact mobile spacing and natural release after the last workspace.

## Operating decisions and external boundaries

- One selected quotation per RFQ; no split award. One order per approved quotation and one invoice per fulfilled order/active contract. Partial payments are supported; installment invoices, credit/debit notes and a general accounting ledger are outside this workflow.
- Catalog availability is maintained by the supplier. Delivery uses partner updates and buyer confirmation; no warehouse stock ledger or carrier feed is represented as integrated.
- The source explicitly permits financial settlement, statutory verification, digital signatures and ERP/accounting functions to be integrated. This implementation tracks payment evidence and staff KYC/BGV decisions; it does not claim a bank transfer, government check or independent background-check result.
- Electronic consent/signing evidence is implemented. A statutory/qualified certificate-based digital signature requires an appropriate selected provider/certificate; it is not substituted with an image of a signature.
- Real inbox delivery requires SMTP credentials/authorized sender or Resend domain/key. SMS is optional in the document and requires a selected gateway. Enterprise SSO/WebAuthn, external video-meeting creation and vendor-specific ERP/BGV adapters require their provider contracts.
- Hosted HTTPS, encrypted infrastructure, malware scanning/quarantine, off-site backups, production monitoring and recovery objectives must be configured and reviewed in the deployment environment. Coordinated encrypted SQLite/PostgreSQL backups include finalized documents and company logos, with integrity/authentication checks and overwrite protection. A live PostgreSQL/private-Blob snapshot and a transaction-rich PostgreSQL fixture have passed separate isolated local restores; this does not certify off-site recovery or a hosted disaster-recovery SLA. See [OPERATIONS.md](OPERATIONS.md).
- Native projects and web behavior are included. Native binaries cannot be built/signed on this machine until full Xcode, a JDK/Android SDK and the organization’s distribution identities are available.

The document’s Microsoft/Coupa/SAP/Oracle/IBM references are design benchmarks, not integrations or affiliations.
